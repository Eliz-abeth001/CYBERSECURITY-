## 1. What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the protection of devices e.g, computers, phones, tablets e.t.c, networks e.g wifi, office internet e.t.c, applications, websites and all other digital from cybercriminals or unauthorized access, attacks, or any digital threat. Cybersecurity is important because almost everything today uses technology e.g banks, school, hospitals....They are many roles under Cybersecurity. It's very broad. 

# 2. Blue Team, Read Team, GRC, Cloud Security and Security Engineering

## I. Blue Team

This team is responsible for defending and protecting systems and attacks. Their work include:

* Monitoring Security Alerts
* Detecting Suspicious Activities
* Investigating Incidents
* Responding To Attacks
* Protecting Systems And Data
* Improving Security Contols

NOTE: They are many other career that does similiar jobs to what is mentioned above e.g SOC Analyst, Incident Responder.......

## II. Red Team

The Red Team the role of an attacker. They are "Ethical Hacker" that are allowed (with permission) to test and find 'weakness' in an organization or any digital security before an attacker does. Their work include:

* Tests Systems
* Simulate Attacks
* Find Weakness
* Performs Ethical Hacking
* Reports Vulnerabilities
The Penetration Tester also does the same thing.

## III. GRC

GRC Means:
* Governance(Making the Rules)
* Risk(Manages Risk)
* Compliance(Following the Rules)

GRC focuses on creating rules, managing risk, ensures the security requirements are followed. They make sure everything stay safe before anything goes wrong. Their work include:

* Writting Security Policies
* Check if anything follow the security rules
* Identify risks before it get so serious
* Help the people follow laws & regulations
* Teach people about cybersecurity best practice.

## IV. Cloud Security

Cloud Security invovles protecting systems, applications, networks and data that are stored through clouds e.g Google Drive, Dropbox, Icloud... Their work include:

* Access Control ( only the right people can access cloud data)
* Identity Management(Encryption..unathorized people can't read the information)
* Data Protection( backups are available if something goes wrong)
* Security Configuration( security setting are configured correctly)
* Threat Detection(weak passwords, stolen accounts....)

## V. Security Engineering

A security Engineer is someone that build and improve security. They don't just wait for attacks, they help prevent them by creating a secured system. A Security Engineer may work on: 

* Firewalls
* Network Security
* Aurhentication Systems
* Security tools
* Access Contols 
* Secure Infrastructure

The focus is on building and maintaining technical security solutions.

# 3. The Duties Of a SOC Analyst

SOC-Security(Protection) Operations(Daily work or activities) Center(A place where people work together)

SOC is a place where cybersecurity professionals monitor and protect an organization's computers, networks and data, 24 hours in a day, 7days a week. They watch; 
* Computers
* Network
* Servers
* Websites
* Cloud Systems.

## Who Is a SOC Analyst?

SOC Analyst is a cybersecurity Professional who "watches, investigate and protect computer systems for signs of attacks and responds when something suspious happen. Their work include;

* Monitor Alerts
* They investigate incident
* They repond to threats 
* They keep record(Documentation)
* They "ESCALATE" serious incident to top tier e.g Level 2 or Level 3 SOC Analyst.

NOTE: A Level 1 SOC Analyst is often one of the first people to examine incoming security alerts. 

## Tools SOC Analyst use;

- Antivirus/Endpoint Protection
- Firewalls
- Threat Intelligence Platform

BASICALLY GRC, BLUE TEAM, RED TEAM AND SOC ANALYST ALL WORK TOGETHER TO PTOTECT BUT HAVE DIFFERNT JOBS.

# 4. Information Security

Also called InfoSec, is the protection of information from being seen by unathorized people. Information Security protect all sort of information no matter where it is. Examples are;

- Passwords, Bank account details, AtmPins, Medical records, School records.....

Cybersecurity is closely related to information security but cybersecurity has a broader focus on protecting digital systems, networks, devices...

# 5. CIA

These are three main goals of information security. 
CIA stand for: 

## Confidentiality

This means only authorized people can have access to information. e.g ATM Pin(my pin is only for me and me alone to know) if someone eles steals my pin then confidentiality is broken.

- How To Protect?

E.g, MFA, Encryption, Access control...

## Intergrity

This means information remain correct, complete and unchanged unless an unathorized person updates it. 

- How To Protect?

E.g, File Permissions, Digital Signatures, Audit logs.

## Availability

This means authorized users can have access to information whenever they need it. 

- How To Protect?

E.g, data backups, reliable servers, power backups..
