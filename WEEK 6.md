Understanding How Logs Work

I started learning how logs work in cybersecurity.

A log is a record of an event or activity that happened on a system.

Logs can contain information such as:

* What happened
* When it happened
* Which user was involved
* Which computer or system was involved
* Which process was involved
* The type of event

Why Logs Matter in Cybersecurity

Security analysts can examine logs to understand activity on a system and investigate events that may require attention.

For example, instead of simply seeing that something happened, an analyst can use the information recorded in a log to investigate what happened, when it happened, and what account or process was involved.
